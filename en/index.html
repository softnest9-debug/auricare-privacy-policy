1. General Information

This Privacy Policy outlines how data is handled in the AuriCare – Hearing Aid App mobile application (hereinafter referred to as the "App"). We respect your privacy and limit data processing strictly to what is required for the proper and secure operation of the App.

The primary audio processing pipeline operates 100% locally on your device—the microphone audio signal is processed directly in the phone's random-access memory (RAM) in real time and is never transmitted or streamed to any external servers.

2. Scope and Methods of Data Processing

  • The App does not collect, profile, or permanently store personally identifiable information (such as name, email address, or phone number).
  • Audio captured via the microphone is used exclusively for real-time sound amplification, noise suppression, and frequency equalization directly on the device.
  • The App does not store voice samples or process conversational content for analytics, advertising, or artificial intelligence model training.
  • The App does not access your GPS location or private data stored by other applications.
  • The App processes only anonymous diagnostic and telemetry data essential for maintaining app stability and improving overall quality (detailed in Section 7).

3. Audio Recording Feature (Local Files)

The App provides an optional feature allowing users to record audio files in WAV format (PCM 48 kHz / 16-bit). Rules regarding recordings:

  • Manual Activation: Recording starts and stops exclusively upon deliberate user action (by tapping the record button in the App). The App never records audio automatically or invisibly in the background.
  • Local Storage: All recorded audio files are saved exclusively in the local storage of your device (within the App's private application directory).
  • No Cloud Transfers: Recorded audio files are never automatically uploaded, synced, or backed up to any external servers or cloud storage solutions.
  • Full User Control: You have full control to play back recordings using the built-in player, permanently delete them from device storage, or manually export them to other apps (such as messaging apps or email clients) using the secure Android system sharing framework (FileProvider).

4. Permissions Required by the App

To function properly, the App requests the following Android system permissions:

  • Microphone / Audio Recording (RECORD_AUDIO, FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MICROPHONE) – Required solely to capture ambient audio, process and amplify it in real time, and save recordings initiated by the user.
  • Nearby Devices / Bluetooth (BLUETOOTH_CONNECT, BLUETOOTH) – Required to establish wireless connections with Bluetooth headphones, hearing aids, and audio accessories.
  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS) – Required to show an ongoing status notification indicating that the background audio processing service is active (an Android system requirement) and to receive informational announcements or update alerts.
  • Wake Lock (WAKE_LOCK) – Required to maintain continuous, uninterrupted real-time DSP audio processing when the device screen turns off.
  • Battery Optimization Exemption (optional, recommended) – The App may prompt you to disable battery optimization restrictions to prevent manufacturer battery-saving mechanisms from prematurely stopping the background audio service.

Denying essential permissions (Microphone, Notifications, Bluetooth) will prevent core features of the App from functioning.

5. Free Trial and Payments

The App offers a 7-day free trial for the PRO tier subscription. Once the trial ends, users can choose between an auto-renewing subscription or a one-time Lifetime license to access full features without limits and ad-free.

All in-app purchases and subscriptions are handled directly and securely through Google Play Billing. We never collect, process, or store credit card details or billing information. For details, visit the Google Privacy Policy.

6. Advertisements and Data Collection

The free version of the App (FREE license) serves ads provided by Google through Google AdMob. For this purpose, the following components are integrated:

  • Google Mobile Ads SDK: This library may collect and process information such as your device's unique advertising identifier (Advertising ID), IP address, ad interaction data, and coarse diagnostics to serve and measure advertisements.
  • User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK: In jurisdictions subject to GDPR/ePrivacy regulations (EEA and the UK), the App uses the UMP SDK to obtain and manage user consent regarding data collection and personalized advertising. Users can revisit and update their privacy choices at any time inside the App settings.

Note: Paid tiers (TRIAL, PRO, PRO LIFETIME) are entirely ad-free, and ad display modules remain completely disabled for these licenses.

Learn more about how Google handles ad data: Google Privacy & Terms – Advertising.

9. Security

We implement strict data security and protection measures:

  • All audio streams are processed entirely on the fly in volatile RAM or stored strictly within the App's protected local sandbox.
  • License states and sensitive preferences are encrypted using Android's hardware-backed KeyStore framework (EncryptedSharedPreferences).
  • Telemetry and configuration exchanges with Google Firebase and Google Play services are conducted exclusively over secure, encrypted HTTPS/TLS connections.
